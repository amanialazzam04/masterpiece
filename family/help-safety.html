Help & Safety

Guidance for getting support and responding when someone may be at risk.

!

IMMEDIATE DANGER

Contact local emergency help.

If you believe someone may act on thoughts of harming themselves or another person, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. If it is safe, stay with them and involve a trusted person nearby.

Nafas is not an emergency service. This portal does not monitor messages or send alerts.

♡

Talk and listen

Ask calmly and directly how they are doing. Listen without judgment and take what they tell you seriously.

✧

Stay connected

Ask what would help right now. Encourage support from a qualified professional or someone they trust.

○

Find local support

Emergency numbers and crisis resources vary by location. Contact local health services or emergency responders for guidance in your area.
